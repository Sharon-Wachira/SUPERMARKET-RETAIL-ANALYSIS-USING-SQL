# SUPERMARKET-RETAIL-ANALYSIS-USING-SQL


A Kenyan retail shop would like to understand the sales performance, customer behaviour and product revenue using transactional data. 

## Data Overview

Our database contained 5 tables
1. Customers which had 200 rows.
2. Orders which had 1484 rows
3. Order_Items
4. Employees had 10 entries. 
5. Products has 30 entries

## Key Insights
- Total revenue:  1,355,293.71
- Electronics is the top category at  573,270 (42% of revenue)
- Nyeri placed the most orders at 74
- M-Pesa ranked last in payment methods with only 89 orders
- 24 customers placed 5 or more orders

## Recommendations
- Investigate why M-Pesa usage is low and run a campaign
- Protect Electronics stock. It drives 42% of revenue
- Monitor the 19% order cancellation rate
